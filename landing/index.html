EK Gallery

An Enemy Kill (EK) is a scored open-world PvP kill — not every death counts. Only kills within the level window count (victim up to ~10 levels below you, or higher). Farming lowbies does not pad your EK score.

Not all EKs are equal. Beyond a raw global EK count, rarity follows how dangerous the victim was on the enemy nation’s killer ladder at the moment of the kill:

  • Legendary — victim was Top 1–10 killers of the other city
  • Rare — victim was Top 11–50
  • Common — victim was Top 51–200

Hunting a legend is worth more prestige than farming the same rank over and over. The hall of fame below is the public screenshot wall of those kills.

Rankings we track (beyond total EKs): most Legendary EKs · most Rare EKs · most total EKs · and nation rivalry boards (Aresden vs Elendiel). Climb the global count and the rarity ladders — different glory for different hunters.

How to install your wallet & play safely

  1. Install Phantom (official only): phantom.app/download — browser extension for Chrome / Brave / Edge / Firefox, or the mobile app. Never install from random ads or DMs.
  2. Create a new wallet (or restore one you already control). Phantom will show a seed phrase (12 or 24 words). That phrase is your account — anyone with it can take everything.
  3. Write the seed words on paper with a pen or pencil — at least two separate copies, stored in different safe places (home safe, family vault, etc.). Do not screenshot, cloud-save, email, or type the phrase into any website or chat.
  4. Never share your seed phrase or private keys — not with “support”, not with admins, not with friends. Helbreath Chain Lords staff will never ask for them.
  5. Unlock Phantom → Play Now on this site → approve the signature (login only; no spend until you choose to). You land on the character list.
  6. Optional: pin Phantom, use a strong device password / hardware wallet for larger holdings, and keep a small “play” wallet separate from long-term storage.

⚠ If you lose every paper copy of your seed words, the wallet cannot be recovered. If someone else gets the words, they own the wallet. Treat paper backups like cash and keys to a vault.

ANNOUNCEMENT · Testing Week 1

Play-mine credits · $HELL at TGE / airdrop

During this first public testing week, every action that earns mining credits feeds a daily pool. At the end of each UTC day, credits are tallied and converted into pending $HELL for that wallet. Redeemable at the Token Generation Event (TGE) / airdrop — not a salary, not ROI.

Credit rules (UTC day):

  • Login — +1 credit (first join of the day).
  • Online / AFK — +10 credits every 4 hours connected (max 6 blocks / day = +60).
  • Monsters — every 100 kills → +10 credits, up to 50 credits from farming (500 kills for the full farm cap).
  • Diversity — kill at least 10 different monster classes in the day → all your credits that day are doubled.
  • Enemy Kills (EK) — +10 credits per eligible EK, max 10 EKs / day (+100). Testing-week EKs do not count toward post-TGE ladders / future EK rewards; credits only.

At day settle, the daily token budget is shared by credit weight among players who were active. If only a few characters play, they still split that day's allocation. Totals roll into pending for TGE.
