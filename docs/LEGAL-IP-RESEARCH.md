### Trademark vs copyright (critical distinction)

Under US law, a mark may be deemed **abandoned** after non-use with intent not to resume; **3 years** non-use is prima facie evidence of abandonment ([15 U.S.C. § 1127](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1127)).  

**Even if** a “Helbreath” trademark were abandoned in a territory:

- Client **graphics**, **maps**, **audio**, **UI chrome**, and **source code** remain copyrightable **expression**.  
- Abandonment of a **mark** does **not** place those works in the **public domain**.  
- Running an “exact copy” client/server still risks **copyright infringement** (and unfair competition / pass-off if consumers think you are the official product).

## 5. Copyright — duration & bankruptcy survival

### United States (illustrative)

- Works created on/after 1978: generally **life + 70**; **works made for hire**: **95 years from publication** or **120 from creation**, whichever shorter ([Copyright Office Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf), revised note 2026).  
- A Korean corporate game from **1999**, if treated as work made for hire in a US analysis, would typically remain protected **well past 2026** (order-of-magnitude into the **2090s** if 95-from-publication applied — **counsel must apply conflict-of-laws**).  
- Bankruptcy/liquidation: IP generally becomes property of the estate / assignees — **not** free scrap for clones (see §3).

### South Korea (illustrative)

- Copyright Act: author’s economic rights generally **life + 70**; **works made for hire** / certain categories often **70 years from making public** (WIPO Lex / Korean Copyright Commission summaries; Lexology overview of KR copyright).  
- Computer programs are protected as copyrighted works. A **1999** published game is **very unlikely** to be free of copyright in Korea in 2026.  
- Continuing official service (2023 press) also undercuts any practical “orphan” narrative.

### Argentina (illustrative)

- **Ley 11.723** (as amended, incl. **Ley 25.036** for software): programs of computación protected; general author term **life + 70** from 1 Jan following death ([InfoLeg text](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/40000-44999/42755/texact.htm); Argentina.gob “derecho fácil” summary).  
- Corporate / anonymous edge cases need local counsel (art. 8-type rules historically discussed for juridical persons — **do not DIY**).

### Community source leaks

Repositories such as [librehelbreath/HGserver](https://github.com/librehelbreath/HGserver) explicitly state they **do not** own original Siementech code and reprint **© 1999–2008 Siementech Co. Ltd.** Availability of leaks **≠** license.

## 7. Exact copy vs “inspired by”

### Exact copy — research answer

| Claim | Verdict in these notes |
|-------|------------------------|
| “Company bankrupt ⇒ free to clone” | **False as a rule.** Copyright usually survives. |
| “Trademark expired ⇒ free to ship same client” | **False.** Mark ≠ copyright. |
| “Private servers do it ⇒ we can ship NFTs with same assets” | **Dangerous.** Non-enforcement is not a license; commercial NFT launch raises visibility. |
| USPTO shows 0 Helbreath marks | **Only** reduces one US **brand** risk factor; does **not** clear copyright. |

### Inspired-by checklist (product work that is less likely wasted)

Treat as **must change / replace** unless counsel clears reuse:

1. **Brand:** word/logo “Helbreath”, classic logos, splash screens, identical trade dress. Prefer **Chain Lord(s)** or other cleared brand.  
2. **City / faction names:** Aresden, Elvine (and close lookalikes used as source identifiers).  
3. **Signature item / spell / monster proper names** that function as source identifiers (e.g. Devastator, Xelima/Merien sets, etc.) — rename or redesign.  
4. **Maps / tilesets / character sprites / UI chrome** taken from original client packs — replace with original or licensed art.  
5. **Leaked C++ server/client** — do not ship; this repo’s **rewritten** C#/TS stack is safer *as code*, but **data/art** cloned from Olympia/classic packs remains risky.  
6. **Mechanics:** high-level PvP city war / isometric ARPG tropes are less distinctive than **specific** numbers, quest text, and identical progression tables — still scrub unique expression.  
7. **Marketing:** avoid “official Helbreath” / “Olympia successor” claims; “inspired by classic 2D city-war MMOs” is safer *as copy tone* (counsel still reviews).

### Olympia-specific notes

| Likely Olympia-owned (if they authored it) | Likely **not** Olympia-owned |
|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Their **custom** code patches, QoL client upgrades they wrote, **Olympia**-branded marketing, original events/items they created from scratch | Original Helbreath **engine heritage**, classic **asset packs**, Siementech **IP**, the word **Helbreath** as game title |
| Their own Discord community goodwill | License to sublicense Helbreath to third parties (no public evidence) |

Olympia (`https://www.helbreath.net/`, domain aged ~2004 per secondary whois aggregators) is widely described as a **long-running private server**, not the Korean rights holder. Using Olympia balance tables as **design reference** ≠ right to redistribute Olympia or classic client binaries/assets.

Upstream README of related base projects already warns: assets remain proprietary to Siementech **or successors** ([ErkoKnoll/helbreath-base-game](https://github.com/ErkoKnoll/helbreath-base-game) README language).
